FASE 6

Preparação
EUA

Entrevistas · Comunicação · Confiança · Entendimento rápido

🎤
Perguntas de Entrevista
  • Tell me about yourself
  • What projects have you built?
  • What technologies do you use?
  • Why backend?
  • Explain this project
// roteiro — tell me about yourself "I'm a backend developer from Brazil. I work with Node.js and PostgreSQL. I've built REST APIs and I'm comfortable with Git and deployment. I'm looking for a remote role where I can grow and contribute to a real product." Adapte com seus dados. Treine até sair natural.
💬
Frases de Reunião

As mais usadas no dia a dia remoto:

  • "Can you repeat that, please?"
  • "Let me check and get back to you."
  • "I'll look into it."
  • "Can you share your screen?"
  • "I'm working on that, should be done by [day]."
  • "I have a question about the ticket."
  • "Sorry, I didn't catch that."
Essas frases aparecem em toda reunião. Decore. Sério.
